How to interpret this: replicate values are averaged at each sampled time. The page compares the mean profile with five approximate 24-hour waveform families (cosine, spike, rigid/triangular, narrow box and wide box) at phases ZT0–ZT23, then reports the highest Pearson correlation. “Oscillating” is an exploratory screen requiring all three cutoffs shown at left. Phase is the fitted model peak relative to dawn (ZT0); stored coordinate 24 h in this condition is ZT0. This browser implementation is HAYSTACK-inspired and is not an exact reproduction of the original DIURNAL 336-model/permutation procedure.
